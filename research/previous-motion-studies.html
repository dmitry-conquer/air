01—02 / ATMOSPHERE

Feel the engineering.

Silk-like currents replace a conventional background. The thermal lens creates a cool pocket around the pointer: the brand literally cools the space.

Next level: airflow emerges from an accurate 3D unit and moves through a beautifully lit interior.

03—04 / MATERIAL

Touch the invisible.

Condensation is a physical metaphor for refrigeration. Particles gather into BREATHE: invisible air takes shape, then scatters at your touch.

Keep condensation as a brief accent. Essential copy and service buttons should remain visible without wiping.

05—06 / BEHAVIOUR

Control the comfort.

Cards respond with local light, gentle tilt and a pressure wave. Scrolling calms the movement and shifts the atmosphere from warm to cool.

Pointer interaction is an enhancement. Touch, keyboard controls and natural scrolling carry the mobile experience.
